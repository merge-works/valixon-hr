The rubric

Seven behaviours that make a dental clinic thrive.

The dimensions were built with practicing dentists. Each staff member is scored on all seven, monthly, by peers and by the owner.

1
Luxury Service Mindset
A premium hospitality mindset: polite, warm, detail-oriented, makes every patient feel specially cared for.
2
Teamwork in Treatment Flow
Coordinates smoothly with the team so treatment flows without friction. Doesn't slow the team down.
3
Patient Emotional Care
Understands patient anxiety, speaks reassuringly, and cares for the emotional side of the visit.
4
Professionalism
Punctual, responsible, polite, and upholds the standards of the clinic every day.
5
Ownership Mindset
Sees problems and helps solve them without being told. Cares about the outcome for the team.
6
Calm Under Pressure
Manages emotions, handles urgent cases and stressful moments professionally.
7
Aesthetic & Detail Orientation
Attention to cleanliness, order, and the premium image of the clinic.
The monthly cycle

Four steps, every month.

Same cadence, same rubric. Different level of hands-on depending on your plan.

1

Send the form

Every team member receives the anonymous monthly evaluation. Same rubric for everyone.

2

Collect peer + CEO ratings

Employees rate their peers anonymously. The owner or CEO submits their own executive scores separately.

3

Choose your weighting

Pick how much weight to give the CEO vs the peer average. Corrects for any bias in either direction.

4

Get your bonus report

Auto-generated summary you can share with the team and use to decide bonuses that same week.
